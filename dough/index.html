From bowl to bake

Mix the ingredients until no dry flour remains. Rest, knead until smooth, then cover and let the dough begin to rise. Divide into balls and refrigerate in covered containers for a cold ferment. Let the dough relax before stretching.

Timing depends on yeast amount and dough temperature. These are general steps for wheat-flour pizza dough; use a trusted recipe for your fermentation schedule. This calculator does not model gluten-free flour.
