DIY Electrolyte Calculator

I'm big into cycling and wanted an easy way to make DIY electrolytes, so here is a simple electrolyte calculator. It uses table sugar, sodium citrate, and potassium chloride, all easy to find at a grocery store, and works out exact amounts for a single dose or a full 5 lb bag.

Weigh sodium citrate and KCl on a 0.1 g (ideally 0.01 g) scale. Mix them into the sugar in stages, then shake or tumble for a few minutes. If the powders settle, run the mix through a blender or sieve once, and give it a shake before scooping.

Informational only, not medical advice. Potassium chloride in excess can be dangerous, especially with kidney disease or certain blood pressure/heart medications. Talk to your doctor if that applies to you, and don't exceed your intended per-dose amounts.
