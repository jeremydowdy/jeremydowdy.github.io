DIY Electrolyte Calculator

I'm big into cycling and wanted an easy way to make DIY electrolytes. Pick a strength and how big a bag of sugar you're starting with, and it gives you the batch recipe plus how much to scoop into each bottle. It uses table sugar, sodium citrate and potassium chloride, all easy to find at a grocery store.

1. Strength per bottle

Custom amounts

2. Batch & flavor

Flavor is about 1 tsp per bottle. Its carbs are taken out of the sugar so each bottle stays on target.

3. Your recipe

Weigh the sodium citrate and potassium chloride on a 0.1 g scale. Mix them into the sugar a bit at a time, then shake the container for a few minutes. Give it a shake before each scoop, since the fine powders can settle.

Informational only, not medical advice. Potassium chloride in excess can be dangerous, especially with kidney disease or certain blood pressure/heart medications. Talk to your doctor if that applies to you, and don't exceed your intended per-bottle amounts.
